## The permit

The Government of South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands (GSGSSI) requires all persons entering the territory to hold an entry permit from 1 August 2025, unless exempt. Applications must be received and paid for at least 8 days before arrival. On a cruise, the operator normally handles this for its passengers as a group. The government updated its fee schedule from 1 July 2026; check the current fee on its permit page.

## The visitor rules

The government's 2026 standard operating procedure for visitors requires, among other things:

- watching the official GSGSSI visitor briefing film before arrival;
- clothing free of seeds and soil, avoiding Velcro, mesh and webbing;
- daypacks and camera bags brushed and vacuumed;
- boot washing before going ashore and again on return to the ship, at every landing;
- at least 5 metres' distance from all wildlife and carcasses.

South Georgia lies about 800 miles south-east of the Falklands. For the voyages that include it, see the [South Georgia and Falklands operator list](/rankings/south-georgia-falklands) and the [route comparison](/compare).
