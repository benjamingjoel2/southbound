"Fly the Drake" is industry shorthand rather than a formal term. It describes trips where the sea crossing of the Drake Passage is replaced by a flight.

## How it works

- IAATO describes air-cruise clients flying from Punta Arenas to King George Island and boarding a ship there. It counted 170 southbound flights into the Teniente Rodolfo Marsh airstrip supporting 126 air-cruise voyages in 2025-26.
- One operator describes a two-hour charter flight. The airline DAP gives about 2.5 hours on its King Air aircraft, depending on the weather.
- One published example itinerary is 8 days and 7 nights, starting and ending in Punta Arenas, with four full days cruising the South Shetlands and the Peninsula.

## The trade-off

Flying saves the sea days and the seasickness risk, but both IAATO and DAP say the programme depends on weather and flight conditions, so departures can be delayed. Compare the routes side by side in the [port and route comparison](/compare).
