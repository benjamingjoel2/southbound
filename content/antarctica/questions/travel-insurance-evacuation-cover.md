## What official advice says

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office advises travellers to Antarctica to make sure that:

- they have appropriate travel insurance that **specifies Antarctica**;
- they have **accessible funds to cover an unexpected medical evacuation**.

It explains why: there are very limited medical facilities in the region, and search and rescue teams called to vessels in Antarctic waters may be far away and take several days to arrive.

IAATO, the tour operators' association, also advises checking that you have adequate medical and travel insurance for your entire journey.

## How much cover

We have not found an official minimum amount that applies to all Antarctic trips, so we do not quote one. Operators set their own requirements; ask yours what it requires before you buy a policy, and check that the policy names Antarctica and covers evacuation.
