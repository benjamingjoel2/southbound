The British Antarctic Survey describes the Snow Hill Island emperor colony, at the edge of the Weddell Sea, as the most northerly emperor breeding site. In November 2023 a survey team reached it using the helicopters of a cruise ship.

Tourist voyages that aim for it are specialist departures on ships carrying helicopters. One operator's published November 2027 departures are 10-night voyages, and it states that helicopter use depends entirely on ice, sea and weather conditions, so a visit is never guaranteed.

Standard Antarctic Peninsula itineraries are built around Adélie, chinstrap and gentoo penguins. See [Is November or January better for penguins?](/questions/november-or-january-for-penguins).
