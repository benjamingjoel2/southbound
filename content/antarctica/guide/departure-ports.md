IAATO reports that most tourism voyages operate in the Antarctic Peninsula region and depart from Ushuaia, Argentina or, to a lesser extent, from other ports north of the Antarctic Treaty area.

## Ushuaia, Argentina

The main port for ship voyages. From Ushuaia, ships cross the Drake Passage to the South Shetland Islands and the Peninsula. See [the Drake Passage](/guide/drake-passage).

## Punta Arenas, Chile: flying to King George Island

For air-cruise (fly-cruise) trips, IAATO says clients fly from Punta Arenas to King George Island in the South Shetlands and board their ship there. IAATO counted 170 southbound flights into the Teniente Rodolfo Marsh airstrip supporting 126 air-cruise voyages in 2025-26. The airline DAP has flown to King George Island since 1989 and gives its flight time as about 2.5 hours, depending on the weather. See [what "fly the Drake" means](/questions/what-does-fly-the-drake-mean).

## Stanley, Falkland Islands

The example Falklands, South Georgia and Peninsula voyage in our comparison starts in Ushuaia and calls at Stanley on the way out.

## New Zealand and Australia

IAATO notes that a few cruises depart from Australia or New Zealand; these go to the Ross Sea side of the continent. One published example sails from Bluff, New Zealand, for 28 days.
