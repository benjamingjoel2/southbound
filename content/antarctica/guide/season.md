IAATO describes the Antarctic Peninsula season as the seven-month austral summer, from October to April. In the 2024-25 season, landings took place from 22 October to 1 April. The [season chart](/season) puts the figures side by side; this page explains them.

## November: ice and courtship

The season's heaviest ice. IAATO reported heavy early-season ice blocking landing sites in 2024-25, so early itineraries are the most likely to change. Penguins are laying: Adélie eggs in mid-November, chinstrap eggs in late November, according to the British Antarctic Survey. In November 2023 a British Antarctic Survey team reached the emperor colony at Snow Hill Island by helicopter from a cruise ship. Mean air temperature at Vernadsky station is about −1.7 °C.

## December: longest days, first chicks

Around the December solstice the sun is up for more than 21 hours at Port Lockroy. Adélie chicks hatch in late December. A study using cruise ships as survey platforms found humpback whale numbers in the Bransfield and Gerlache Straits rising rapidly through December.

## January: chicks everywhere

Chinstrap chicks hatch in early January; Adélie chicks gather in crèches at about three weeks old. The same whale study found humpback numbers stable by mid-January. January and February have the warmest monthly means at Peninsula stations, about +1 to +1.6 °C.

## February: chicks leaving, ice at its minimum

Adélie chicks are at sea by mid-February. Continent-wide, sea ice reaches its yearly minimum in the southern summer (on 20 February in 2024), which is why voyages that try to cross the Antarctic Circle run later in the season.

## March: the end of the season

Daylight drops to under 13 hours by mid-March. Chinstrap chicks have usually fledged about two months after hatching. Researchers assume humpback whales remain in high numbers from mid-December into late April.

## Gentoo penguins

Gentoo breeding is not well synchronised, so colonies can be at different stages at the same time. That is why the chart gives no month-by-month gentoo dates.
